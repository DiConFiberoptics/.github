## Photonics, from components to systems

DiCon develops MEMS optical switching systems, fiber-optic components and solutions for photonic device testing. Our capabilities span chip design, manufacturing, assembly and software integration, with operations in the United States and Taiwan.

**從元件到系統，專注光子技術。** DiCon 致力於 MEMS 光學交換、光纖元件與光子元件測試解決方案，整合晶片設計、製造、組裝與軟體技術。
