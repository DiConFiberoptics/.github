## Brand animation

[profile/assets/dicon-gfi-brand.gif](profile/assets/dicon-gfi-brand.gif) combines the original DiCon logo artwork with motion from the existing [GFI / DiCon corporate profile](https://gfi-apps.diconcloud.com/gfi-corporate-profile/), version 1.4.0. The silent loop emphasizes DiCon Fiberoptics with large opening and closing brand cards; no AI redraw was used.

- Sequence: DiCon Fiberoptics brand card (5 seconds), original Global Fiberoptics opening 00:03–00:07.5 (4.5 seconds), then DiCon Fiberoptics brand card (5 seconds).
- Output: 14.5 seconds, 800 × 450 pixels, 10 fps, 96-color GIF, approximately 2.3 MiB.
- Original white DiCon logo artwork is preserved at its native aspect ratio. Brand cards use the opening's light motion over a navy background, the full DiCon Fiberoptics name, the existing company tagline and official website.
- Exported with the source project's film renderer, then composited, concatenated and palette-encoded using FFmpeg.
- Keep the image and its caption linked to the full corporate profile. Do not embed scripts or the full player in the organization README.
